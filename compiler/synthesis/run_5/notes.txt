Candidate 1 hypothesis: preserve incumbent preparation for goals with deeper
travel or iron/diamond needs. For structurally shallow simple resource tasks,
use minimal pick preparation so coal reserves cannot trap later unnecessary
crafting. After reserves activate, directly pursue descent and use frontier
search when the ladder is unknown; preserve incumbent descent on reserve-free
reach stages. Increase explicit resource search radius to 1000. Base survival
rules remain earlier and the original calm/health/supply gate remains.

699477 actual steps. SHA256 432e7df5596c239893c66dfaa2be81839c56bdeb27251384043e05895c85bf49.
Shallow coal goals both became 4/4. Direct reserved-stage initial descent lost
successes on wood/stone maintenance goals and a wood/iron goal; survival
climbs and subsequent descents oscillated on some worlds. This supports

Candidate 2: retain the structural shallow path. Restore resource radius 300.
Replace proactive reserved-stage descent with positive-reserve top-up margins
(wood 3, stone 2, coal/iron/diamond 3). This lets frozen recipe and station
choices spend surplus while the guarded minimum survives; default deeper
preparation, provisioning and descent remain in charge.

Candidate 3: while reserves are active on deeper reach stages, replenish wood
up to reserve+4 even if its reserve is zero, covering stations and a recipe.
Search remembered deposits with radius 1000. If replenishment cannot find a
source, explicitly search frontiers instead of immediately exposing guarded
crafting. Wood absence below floor 0 routes upward; ore absence on cleared
floor 1 with suitable equipment can route toward floor 2. Hunting visible
prey on uncleared floors preserves clearing opportunities during resource
search. Pure reach behavior and shallow structural preparation remain.
